Archived Policy Reform Files
----------------------------

Before the merge of [pull request
3225](https://github.com/PSLmodels/Tax-Calculator/pull/3225), this
folder contained old tax reforms, most of which were of little current
interest and many of which could no longer be simulated without
[structural
enhancements](https://github.com/PSLmodels/Tax-Calculator/blob/master/agents/README.md).

If you want to look at these old reforms, consult the above mentioned PR.
